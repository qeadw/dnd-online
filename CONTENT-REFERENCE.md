# D&D 5e Content Reference Guide

This document outlines all content to be added from the three most popular D&D 5e sourcebooks.

## Source Books

### 1. Player's Handbook (PHB)
The core rulebook containing base classes, races, and spells.

### 2. Monster Manual (MM)
The core monster compendium with 400+ creatures.

### 3. Xanathar's Guide to Everything (XGtE)
Popular expansion with subclasses, spells, and optional rules.

---

## PART 1: CLASSES & SUBCLASSES

### Player's Handbook Classes (12 Base Classes)

#### 1. Barbarian
- **Hit Die:** d12
- **Primary Ability:** Strength
- **Saves:** Strength, Constitution
- **Subclasses (Primal Paths):**
  - Path of the Berserker (PHB)
  - Path of the Totem Warrior (PHB)
  - Path of the Ancestral Guardian (XGtE)
  - Path of the Storm Herald (XGtE)
  - Path of the Zealot (XGtE)

#### 2. Bard
- **Hit Die:** d8
- **Primary Ability:** Charisma
- **Saves:** Dexterity, Charisma
- **Subclasses (Colleges):**
  - College of Lore (PHB)
  - College of Valor (PHB)
  - College of Glamour (XGtE)
  - College of Swords (XGtE)
  - College of Whispers (XGtE)

#### 3. Cleric
- **Hit Die:** d8
- **Primary Ability:** Wisdom
- **Saves:** Wisdom, Charisma
- **Subclasses (Divine Domains):**
  - Knowledge Domain (PHB)
  - Life Domain (PHB)
  - Light Domain (PHB)
  - Nature Domain (PHB)
  - Tempest Domain (PHB)
  - Trickery Domain (PHB)
  - War Domain (PHB)
  - Forge Domain (XGtE)
  - Grave Domain (XGtE)

#### 4. Druid
- **Hit Die:** d8
- **Primary Ability:** Wisdom
- **Saves:** Intelligence, Wisdom
- **Subclasses (Circles):**
  - Circle of the Land (PHB)
  - Circle of the Moon (PHB)
  - Circle of Dreams (XGtE)
  - Circle of the Shepherd (XGtE)

#### 5. Fighter
- **Hit Die:** d10
- **Primary Ability:** Strength or Dexterity
- **Saves:** Strength, Constitution
- **Subclasses (Martial Archetypes):**
  - Champion (PHB)
  - Battle Master (PHB)
  - Eldritch Knight (PHB)
  - Arcane Archer (XGtE)
  - Cavalier (XGtE)
  - Samurai (XGtE)

#### 6. Monk
- **Hit Die:** d8
- **Primary Ability:** Dexterity & Wisdom
- **Saves:** Strength, Dexterity
- **Subclasses (Monastic Traditions):**
  - Way of the Open Hand (PHB)
  - Way of Shadow (PHB)
  - Way of the Four Elements (PHB)
  - Way of the Drunken Master (XGtE)
  - Way of the Kensei (XGtE)
  - Way of the Sun Soul (XGtE)

#### 7. Paladin
- **Hit Die:** d10
- **Primary Ability:** Strength & Charisma
- **Saves:** Wisdom, Charisma
- **Subclasses (Sacred Oaths):**
  - Oath of Devotion (PHB)
  - Oath of the Ancients (PHB)
  - Oath of Vengeance (PHB)
  - Oath of Conquest (XGtE)
  - Oath of Redemption (XGtE)

#### 8. Ranger
- **Hit Die:** d10
- **Primary Ability:** Dexterity & Wisdom
- **Saves:** Strength, Dexterity
- **Subclasses (Conclaves):**
  - Hunter (PHB)
  - Beast Master (PHB)
  - Gloom Stalker (XGtE)
  - Horizon Walker (XGtE)
  - Monster Slayer (XGtE)

#### 9. Rogue
- **Hit Die:** d8
- **Primary Ability:** Dexterity
- **Saves:** Dexterity, Intelligence
- **Subclasses (Archetypes):**
  - Thief (PHB)
  - Assassin (PHB)
  - Arcane Trickster (PHB)
  - Inquisitive (XGtE)
  - Mastermind (XGtE)
  - Scout (XGtE)
  - Swashbuckler (XGtE)

#### 10. Sorcerer
- **Hit Die:** d6
- **Primary Ability:** Charisma
- **Saves:** Constitution, Charisma
- **Subclasses (Origins):**
  - Draconic Bloodline (PHB)
  - Wild Magic (PHB)
  - Divine Soul (XGtE)
  - Shadow Magic (XGtE)
  - Storm Sorcery (XGtE)

#### 11. Warlock
- **Hit Die:** d8
- **Primary Ability:** Charisma
- **Saves:** Wisdom, Charisma
- **Subclasses (Patrons):**
  - The Archfey (PHB)
  - The Fiend (PHB)
  - The Great Old One (PHB)
  - The Celestial (XGtE)
  - The Hexblade (XGtE)

#### 12. Wizard
- **Hit Die:** d6
- **Primary Ability:** Intelligence
- **Saves:** Intelligence, Wisdom
- **Subclasses (Arcane Traditions):**
  - School of Abjuration (PHB)
  - School of Conjuration (PHB)
  - School of Divination (PHB)
  - School of Enchantment (PHB)
  - School of Evocation (PHB)
  - School of Illusion (PHB)
  - School of Necromancy (PHB)
  - School of Transmutation (PHB)
  - War Magic (XGtE)
